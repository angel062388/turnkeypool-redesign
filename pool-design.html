04 · Options

Pool Design Options

Option · 01

Above-Ground Pool

There are several reasons why above-ground pools are a preferred choice for many homeowners. First, they are more affordable compared to in-ground pools, making them a budget-friendly option.

Additionally, above-ground pools are easier and quicker to install, which means you can start enjoying your pool sooner.

Option · 02

Architectural Pool

Architectural pools are designed with a focus on the overall aesthetic and architectural style of your home. They are often custom-designed to seamlessly integrate with the surrounding landscape and enhance the overall visual appeal of your outdoor space.

With architectural pools, you have the freedom to choose from a variety of shapes, sizes, and features that best complement your home's design.

Option · 03

Recreational Swimming Pool

Homeowners can choose to have a traditional rectangular pool, which is a popular and classic choice. This design allows for easy pool activities and fits well in most backyard spaces.

Another option is a freeform pool, which offers a more natural and organic shape. This design is great for homeowners who want a pool that blends seamlessly with the surrounding landscape.

Option · 04

Indoor Pool

When it comes to pool design options, one popular choice is an indoor pool. An indoor pool offers several advantages to homeowners.

It can be enjoyed year-round, regardless of the weather conditions outside. Additionally, an indoor pool provides privacy and can be a great way to exercise and relax without worrying about prying eyes or external disturbances.

Option · 05

Infinity Pool

These pools create the illusion of water extending to the horizon, giving the impression that the pool seamlessly merges with the surrounding landscape or body of water.

Infinity pools enhance the aesthetic appeal of any property and provide a luxurious and stylish atmosphere.

Option · 06

Kiddie Pool

A kiddie pool is a smaller and shallower pool designed specifically for young children. It provides a safe and enjoyable environment for kids to play and splash around in.

The size and depth of a kiddie pool can be customized to meet the needs and preferences of the homeowners.

Option · 07

Lap Pool

Lap pools are rectangular in shape and are designed specifically for swimming laps and other aquatic exercises.

These pools typically have a length of 50 to 75 feet and a width of 8 to 10 feet, providing enough space for swimmers to move comfortably.

Option · 08

Spool

A spool is a smaller-sized pool, typically around 12–14 feet long and 6–8 feet wide, designed to provide the benefits of both a pool and a spa.

This option is popular for homeowners who have limited backyard space but still want to enjoy the relaxation and hydrotherapy benefits of a spa.
