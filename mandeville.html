06 · The Area

All About Mandeville, LA

About Mandeville

Steeped in history and Southern charm, Mandeville, Louisiana offers a vibrant lifestyle on the shores of Lake Pontchartrain. Founded in 1834, the city boasts a rich past, evident in its connection to the prominent Marigny family. Once a summer escape for New Orleanians seeking respite from the city's heat, Mandeville has blossomed into a thriving community. Today, it attracts residents with its scenic location, excellent schools, and strong sense of community.

Mandeville's cultural scene thrives with museums, art galleries, and historical sites. Outdoor enthusiasts enjoy exploring the Tammany Trace, a 31-mile rail trail perfect for walking, biking, or picnicking. The Mandeville Lakefront bustles with activity, hosting events like the renowned Jazz Festival and the Christmas Boat Parade, a dazzling display of holiday cheer. Whether you seek cultural immersion, outdoor adventure, or family-friendly fun, Mandeville offers something for everyone.

Things to Do

Step Back in Time: Explore Mandeville's fascinating past at museums and historical sites. Learn about the city's connection to the prominent Marigny family or delve into the region's rich heritage.

Embrace the Arts: Mandeville's art scene flourishes with galleries showcasing local talent. Attend the Mandeville Art Walk to discover unique pieces and meet the artists.

Thrill Your Senses: Catch a live performance at The Mandeville Trailhead or delve into the world of science at the EcoTarium.

Become One with Nature: Hike or bike along the scenic Tammany Trace, a 31-mile rail trail offering stunning views and a chance to commune with nature.

Nearby Suburbs

Folsom: Known for its excellent schools, strong sense of community, and abundance of family-friendly parks and recreation facilities.

Covington: Offers a vibrant downtown area with shops, restaurants, and a historic atmosphere. Covington is also home to excellent public schools and plenty of green space for outdoor activities.

Abita Springs: A more rural setting with a strong focus on education and a peaceful environment. Abita Springs boasts the popular Abita Brewing Company, a perfect spot for adult refreshment after a day of exploring.

Slidell: A growing community with a mix of urban and suburban living, offering a variety of restaurants, shops, and nightlife options. Slidell is also known for its proximity to major highways and its convenient location for commuters.

Interesting/Fun Facts

Cupid's Factory: Believe it or not, Mandeville can claim the title of "Cupid's Factory"! In the mid-1800s, a resourceful young woman named Esther Howland started the very first commercial production of valentines in the US right here in Mandeville.

Almost a Rocket City: Space enthusiasts might be surprised to learn that Mandeville has a connection to rocket science. Robert Goddard, considered the "Father of Modern Rocketry," conducted his groundbreaking experiments with liquid-fueled rockets while teaching physics at Clark University in Mandeville.

A City With a Heart: Forget boring city symbols! Mandeville's official symbol is a heart. This reflects the city's welcoming spirit and its role as a regional medical center.

A Nickname with a Twist: Back in the 1970s, Mandeville had a nickname with an unexpected twist – "Wormtown." However, it wasn't meant as a compliment! A local DJ supposedly coined it due to the lack of a prominent punk rock scene at the time. Thankfully, the residents of Mandeville have embraced the nickname with a touch of humor and turned it into a badge of local pride.

Public Transportation

STAR Transit: The St. Tammany Parish Government operates STAR Transit, which offers limited bus service within Mandeville and connects to other parts of St. Tammany Parish.

Ridesharing services: Uber and Lyft are readily available in Mandeville. These can be a convenient option for getting around town, especially for short trips or if buses aren't running at your desired time.

Taxis: Traditional taxi services like Yellow Cab may be available in Mandeville, but they might be less prevalent than ridesharing options.
