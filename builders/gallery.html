Our Process

We Design and Build, You Create Memories

  1. 1

    Initial Consultation

    Our free consultation helps us understand your vision - we'll schedule an expert visit to assess your needs and measure your space for a perfect pool design.

  2. 2

    Design and Planning

    See your ideal pool in 3D, choose the ideal materials, and let us handle the permit approval process. We'll finalize all details for a smooth start.

  3. 3

    Pre-Construction Meeting

    We start with a pre-construction meeting. We walk the site with you, confirm the final layout and finishes, and go through what happens at each stage of the build.

  4. 4

    Excavation and Build

    Then we excavate and build with expertise. Steel, plumbing and the gunite shell go in stage by stage, with disruption to your property kept to a minimum.

  5. 5

    Testing and Handover

    We test everything, and finally hand over your dream pool! We run every system, then walk you through how your pool and equipment work day to day.
