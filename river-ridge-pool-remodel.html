05 · Popular Services

Popular Pool Remodeling Services

Pool resurfacing and plastering

Many pool owners choose to have their pools resurfaced and plastered to enhance the visual appeal and durability of their pools. Over time, the surface of a pool can become worn, cracked, or faded, making it less appealing and potentially causing leaks. Pool resurfacing involves removing the old surface and applying a new one, while plastering involves applying a layer of plaster to the pool's surface.

Tile and coping replacement

Over time, the tiles and coping around a pool can become outdated, worn, or damaged. This not only affects the visual appeal of the pool but can also pose safety hazards. By replacing the tiles and coping, pool owners can give their pool a fresh, updated look while ensuring that it is safe and functional.

Pool deck and patio renovation

Pool deck and patio renovation can include various elements such as repairing or replacing cracked or damaged tiles, resurfacing the deck or patio with new materials, adding decorative elements like stamped concrete or pavers, and installing additional features like fire pits or outdoor kitchens.

Equipment upgrades and automation

Upgrading pool equipment such as filters, pumps, and heaters can greatly improve the efficiency and effectiveness of the pool. Automation is another sought-after service that allows pool owners to control various pool functions, such as temperature, lighting, and water features, through a smartphone or tablet.

06 · Benefits

Benefits of Pool Remodeling

Enhancing aesthetics and curb appeal

Over time, pools can become outdated and worn out, which can detract from the overall appearance of the property. By remodeling the pool, homeowners can rejuvenate its appearance and create a more inviting and attractive space. Whether it's adding new tile, updating the pool deck, or installing modern water features, pool remodeling can transform the pool area into a visually appealing and stylish outdoor living space.

Increasing property value

Updating and renovating a pool can greatly enhance the overall appeal and attractiveness of a property, making it more desirable to potential buyers. A well-maintained and modernized pool can serve as a major selling point and differentiate a property from others on the market.

Improving functionality and safety

By remodeling the pool, you can update its design and add modern features that enhance its functionality. This can include adding new water features, upgrading the filtration system, or installing energy-efficient equipment. Additionally, pool remodeling can also address safety concerns.

Saving on long-term maintenance costs

Over time, pools can develop cracks, leaks, or outdated equipment, which can lead to increased maintenance and repair expenses. By remodeling the pool, you can address these issues and install more efficient and modern equipment that can save you money in the long run.
