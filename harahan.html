Indoor Pool

An indoor pool offers a multitude of benefits, especially in a location where the weather can be unpredictable or during the colder months. It allows for year-round enjoyment and exercise, regardless of the outside temperature or weather conditions. Indoor pools also provide a level of privacy and convenience, as they can be easily accessed from within your home or a dedicated indoor space. 03

Infinity Pool

Infinity pools are known for their stunning visual effect, as the water appears to blend seamlessly with the horizon. These pools are designed with a vanishing edge, where the water spills over into a catch basin and is then recirculated back into the pool. They create a luxurious and sophisticated look, perfect for homeowners looking to enhance their outdoor living space and create a resort-like oasis in their own backyard.

04

Kiddie Pool

A kiddie pool is a small, shallow pool that is specifically designed for young children to enjoy. It is a safe and fun option for families with young kids who want to provide them with a safe water play area. Our kiddie pools are built with durable and child-friendly materials, ensuring that they are secure and long-lasting. We also offer a range of customization options for the kiddie pool design, such as adding water features, slides, and interactive elements to make it even more enjoyable for children.

07 · The Area

All About Harahan, LA

About Harahan

Craving a charming community with big-city access? Look no further than Harahan, Louisiana. Nestled within Jefferson Parish, Harahan offers a friendly atmosphere and a strong sense of community. This "City of Friendship" boasts a population of around 9,000, fostering a close-knit feel. Residents enjoy the peace and quiet of suburban life, while still having easy access to the excitement and amenities of New Orleans just a short drive away.

Harahan is a popular choice for families due to its good schools, safe environment, and abundance of parks and green spaces. Whether you're seeking historical significance, delicious food options, or simply a welcoming place to call home, Harahan offers a delightful blend for all to enjoy.

Things To Do

Destrehan Plantation: This historic mansion, built in 1779, offers tours, carriage rides, and swamp tours, showcasing life on a Lower Mississippi Valley plantation.

Lafitte Greenway: This 2.6-mile linear park is perfect for walks, bike rides, or runs. It also features playgrounds and dog parks along the way.

Celebration in the Oaks (New Orleans): Immerse yourself in the holiday spirit with this annual light festival at City Park, featuring over two million dazzling lights.

Elmwood Shopping Center: This outdoor mall boasts over 100 stores and restaurants, offering a variety of shopping and dining options.

Notable Residents

Tommy Wilcox: an American television personality and former college football player.

John Mosca (pronounced "Mohsca"): was an American restaurateur and owner (and co-founder) of the famed Mosca's, a Louisiana Creole and Italian restaurant in Avondale, Louisiana, near New Orleans.[

Francis Edward Lauricella, known as Hank Lauricella: was an American real estate developer from suburban New Orleans, Louisiana, a college football legend, and a member of both houses of the Louisiana State Legislature.

Johnny Arthur Giavotella: is an American former professional baseball second baseman. He played in Major League Baseball (MLB) for the Kansas City Royals, Los Angeles Angels, and Baltimore Orioles.

Interesting / Fun Facts

Three Largest Barges Ever Built: Believe it or not, Harahan was once home to the three largest barges ever constructed at the time – the Mastodon, Mammoth, and El Grande. These behemoths were built in the early 1900s and used to transport massive cargo across the Mississippi River.

Experimental Farm: The land where Harahan now sits was once part of the larger Soniat Plantation. In the late 1800s, a portion of the former plantation was used by Southern University and A&M College as an experimental farm.

Not Originally Called Harahan: The city wasn't always known as Harahan. Before 1910, it was referred to as "Soniat Station" or "Harahan Junction." The name change came about to honor James T. Harahan, the former president of the Illinois Central Railroad Company, which played a significant role in the city's development.

Ferry Crossing for Trains: Prior to the construction of the Huey P. Long Bridge, Harahan was one of the few points where railcars could cross the Mississippi River. The Illinois Central and Southern Pacific Railroads maintained a ferry crossing between Harahan and Avondale specifically for transporting railcars across the mighty river.

Public Transportation in Harahan

JP Transit: offering several routes that connect Harahan to various destinations within Jefferson Parish and beyond.

Ridesharing Services: Ridesharing services like Uber or Lyft might be another option for getting around Harahan and the surrounding areas.

Taxis: While less common, taxis might also be available for hire in Harahan.
