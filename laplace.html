All About Laplace, LA

About Laplace, LA

LaPlace, pronounced lə-PLAHSS, is a designated census area in St. John the Baptist Parish, Louisiana, USA. Positioned on the eastern side of the Mississippi River, it forms part of the greater New Orleans metropolitan region. As of 2020, its population stood at 28,841.

Serving as the endpoint for Interstate 55, LaPlace connects with Interstate 10, and also marks the termination of US 51, where it intersects with US 61. Situated 25 miles (40 km) to the west of New Orleans, LaPlace enjoys a strategic location in the region.

Interesting/Fun Facts About Laplace, LA

Andouille, a smoked pork sausage with roots in France, is well-loved in LaPlace and across Louisiana. However, in the 1970s, Governor Edwin Edwards officially declared LaPlace as the "Andouille Capital of the World." Since 1972, LaPlace has celebrated its Andouille heritage with an annual Andouille Festival held every October.

Public Transportation

Laplace, Louisiana provides ample opportunities for residents to enjoy living, working, and leisure activities. While lacking its own public transit system, the city offers various transportation alternatives. Jefferson Transit's bus service is particularly convenient for locals, serving Laplace and neighboring areas within the greater New Orleans Region. Moreover, multiple taxi companies cater to both short and long-distance travel needs, including airport transfers and trips to nearby cities. Additionally, the accessibility of ride-sharing platforms like Uber and Lyft simplifies navigation for visitors, eliminating the need for rental cars or reliance on public transit. With these diverse options, traversing Laplace sans personal vehicle is effortless.
