03 · Benefits

Benefits of Pool Remodeling

New Orleans' vibrant climate practically begs for a refreshing dip year-round, but a tired pool can detract from the fun. Don't despair! Pool remodeling offers a multitude of benefits that can transform your outdated oasis into a stunning and functional centerpiece of your outdoor living space.

Here's how a New Orleans pool remodel can enhance your property:

  • 01

    Enhancing aesthetics and curb appeal

    One of the benefits of pool remodeling in New Orleans is enhancing the aesthetics and curb appeal of the pool area. Over time, pools can become outdated and worn out, which can detract from the overall appearance of the property. By remodeling the pool, homeowners can rejuvenate its appearance and create a more inviting and attractive space. Whether it's adding new tile, updating the pool deck, or installing modern water features, pool remodeling can transform the pool area into a visually appealing and stylish outdoor living space.

  • 02

    Increasing property value

    One of the major benefits of pool remodeling in New Orleans is the increase in property value. Updating and renovating a pool can greatly enhance the overall appeal and attractiveness of a property, making it more desirable to potential buyers. A well-maintained and modernized pool can serve as a major selling point and differentiate a property from others on the market.

  • 03

    Improving functionality and safety

    Over time, pools can become outdated and their features may start to deteriorate. By remodeling the pool, you can update its design and add modern features that enhance its functionality. This can include adding new water features, upgrading the filtration system, or installing energy-efficient equipment. Additionally, pool remodeling can also address safety concerns. Outdated pool structures may have cracks or uneven surfaces that can pose a risk to swimmers. By remodeling, you can fix any structural issues and ensure that the pool is safe for use.

  • 04

    Saving on long-term maintenance costs

    Over time, pools can develop cracks, leaks, or outdated equipment, which can lead to increased maintenance and repair expenses. By remodeling the pool, you can address these issues and install more efficient and modern equipment that can save you money in the long run. For example, upgrading to energy-efficient pool pumps or installing a pool cover can help reduce energy consumption and decrease the amount of water and chemicals needed for maintenance.

04 · Popular Services

Popular Pool Remodeling Services in New Orleans

Does your once-pristine New Orleans pool no longer reflect the elegance and functionality you desire? Perhaps the passage of time has manifested in the form of faded plaster, outdated tile, or a pool deck in need of refurbishment.

Elevate your outdoor living space with some of the most sought-after pool remodeling services available:

01

Pool resurfacing and plastering

One of the popular pool services in New Orleans is pool resurfacing and plastering. Many pool owners choose to have their pools resurfaced and plastered to enhance the visual appeal and durability of their pools. Over time, the surface of a pool can become worn, cracked, or faded, making it less appealing and potentially causing leaks. Pool resurfacing involves removing the old surface and applying a new one, while plastering involves applying a layer of plaster to the pool's surface.

02

Tile and coping replacement

One of the most popular pool remodeling services in New Orleans is tile and coping replacement. Over time, the tiles and coping around a pool can become outdated, worn, or damaged. This not only affects the visual appeal of the pool but can also pose safety hazards. By replacing the tiles and coping, pool owners can give their pool a fresh, updated look while ensuring that it is safe and functional. Tile and coping replacement services typically involve removing the old tiles and coping, preparing the surface, and installing new, high-quality tiles and coping materials.

03

Pool deck and patio renovation

One of the most popular pool remodeling services in New Orleans is pool deck and patio renovation. This service is sought after by pool owners who want to enhance the overall aesthetic appeal and functionality of their pools. Pool deck and patio renovation can include various elements such as repairing or replacing cracked or damaged tiles, resurfacing the deck or patio with new materials, adding decorative elements like stamped concrete or pavers, and installing additional features like fire pits or outdoor kitchens.

04

Equipment upgrades and automation

Upgrading pool equipment such as filters, pumps, and heaters can greatly improve the efficiency and effectiveness of the pool. This not only enhances the overall pool experience but also helps in reducing energy consumption and maintenance costs. Automation is another sought-after service that allows pool owners to control various pool functions, such as temperature, lighting, and water features, through a smartphone or tablet.
